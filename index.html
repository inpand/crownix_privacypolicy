Privacy Policy for Crownix

Application: Crownix: Chess Battles (com.inpand.crownix)
Developer: Inpand Works
Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Inpand Works ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, secure gaming experience. This Privacy Policy outlines how the Crownix: Chess Battles mobile application ("Crownix" or the "App") handles user information across the Google Play platform.

1. Zero Personal Data Collected

Crownix is designed from the ground up with privacy by design:

  • No Account Required: You can play all modes without creating an account, registering, or providing an email address, phone number, real name, or password.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We never request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, device photos, files, precise GPS location, or personal sensors.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: We do not track you across other applications, nor do we collect advertising identifiers (GAID).
  • No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker any user data to data brokers or third parties.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All your progress, achievements, and settings remain stored strictly in your device's private local application sandbox:

  • Gameplay Progress: Completed Kingdom Adventure stages, tactical puzzle solution badges (Gold, Silver, Bronze, Defender), win/loss/draw records against AI opponents, and daily login streaks.
  • In-Game Virtual Currency: Crownix Coins balance and unlocked visual board/piece cosmetics. Crownix Coins are purely non-monetary, virtual game counters earned solely via gameplay.
  • Customization Preferences: Your chosen player alias (default: "Royal Challenger"), active board theme, sound effects settings, and haptic vibration preferences.
Your Data Deletion Rights: Because all game data is stored exclusively on your local device, you can permanently delete all data at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Crownix > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.

3. Android System Permissions Used

Permission Technical Name Why It Is Needed
Internet Access android.permission.INTERNET Reserved for future online room multiplayer features. All current gameplay modes run 100% offline on your device.
Network State android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks network connectivity to display offline status indicators.
Vibration android.permission.VIBRATE Provides optional tactile haptic feedback when picking up, moving, or capturing chess pieces. Can be disabled in Settings.

4. Offline Operation & Future Online Features

Crownix is designed primarily as a standalone, on-device chess application:

  • Current Offline Experience: All AI opponents, 50 Kingdom stages, Daily Puzzles, and two-player matches execute entirely on your device without transmitting data over the internet.
  • Future Online Updates: If online private room matches are enabled in future updates, data transmission will be strictly limited to transient move coordinates (e.g., e2e4) and clock timers over encrypted connections. No user accounts, chat logs, or personal profiles will ever be collected or stored.

5. Third-Party Services & Ad Networks

Crownix does not include third-party advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads) and does not embed third-party analytics tracking SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Facebook Graph SDK, AppsFlyer).

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Crownix is safe and family-friendly for players of all ages:

  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • The game contains no open chat or social messaging features that could expose children to external contact.
  • If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted any personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will delete it.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game enhancements or legal adjustments. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated Effective Date.
